## 4. The perceptions of task cohesion in collaborative learning teams
- ID: `2024_v19i3_10.1007_s11412-024-09424-5_The_perceptions_of_task_cohesion_in_collaborative_learning_teams`
- Year: 2024 | Volume: 19 | Issue: 3 | Article number: 5
- Authors: Andrew Zamecnik & Georg Grossmann & Djazia Ladjal & Abelardo Pardo
- General summary:
  - Researchers analyzed team interaction data using temporal motif analysis to investigate changes in task cohesion among collaborative learning teams. The study found that teams with strong task cohesion and high performance tend to share similar cohesive perceptions, while those with different cohesion perceptions have lower interdependence and poorer performance. This research provides insights into promoting positive adjustments aligning team perceptions, enhancing collaborative learning, and supporting improved performance and engagement among teams.
- Trend coding draft:
  - methodology_primary: Mixed-methods approach
  - unit_of_analysis_primary: Collaborative learning teams
  - pedagogy_primary: Task cohesion-focused collaborative learning
  - technology_primary: Temporal network motifs and surveys
  - theory_primary: Cohesion theory (Festinger, 1950)
  - ai_llm_involvement_primary: None mentioned
  - evidence_span: Longitudinal study with two assessment points
  - coding_confidence: High
  - coding_notes: Task cohesion is a critical component of team performance and learning outcomes. Temporal network motifs provide a valuable means to capture the dynamics of cohesion within teams.
